TRADEEASE PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: September 25, 2026.| Last Updated: September 25, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how TradeEase (“TradeEase”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, stores and protects personal information when you use the TradeEase website, mobile/PWA application, marketplace, vendor tools, payment features, communications and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

TradeEase is a marketplace that may connect customers with independent vendors offering products and/or services. Depending on the transaction, TradeEase may act as a marketplace operator, data controller, data processor, or another legally recognised role. Vendors may independently process customer information in connection with fulfilling orders and their own legal obligations.

For users in Nigeria, TradeEase will comply with applicable requirements of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and relevant regulatory guidance. Where laws such as the GDPR apply because of a user’s location or circumstances, TradeEase will apply the applicable requirements to that processing.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: name, username, email address, telephone number, password credentials or authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information: profile photo, business name, vendor description, business/contact details, preferences and other information you choose to publish.
  • Order and transaction information: products/services ordered, quantities, prices, delivery information, order status, invoices, transaction references, refunds and related communications.
  • Vendor information: business registration or verification information, store details, product/service listings, payout information, tax or regulatory information where required, and documents submitted for verification.
  • Customer support information: messages, complaints, reviews, requests, attachments and records of interactions with support.
  • Marketing preferences: subscriptions, notification choices and responses to promotions or surveys.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical data such as IP address, browser/device type, operating system, language, approximate location derived from technical information, unique identifiers and diagnostic information.
  • Usage data such as pages/screens visited, searches, clicks, session information, referring pages, marketplace interactions and feature usage.
  • Security data such as authentication events, access logs, fraud/security signals and records needed to protect the Services.

2.3 Information from third parties

  • Authentication providers when you choose social sign-in.
  • Payment providers and financial institutions for payment status, transaction references and fraud/security information. TradeEase does not intend to store full payment-card details when those details are handled directly by a payment processor.
  • Vendors, delivery partners, verification providers, analytics/service providers and other parties where lawful and necessary to provide the Services.
  • Publicly available or legally obtained business information where relevant to vendor verification or marketplace integrity.

3. Sensitive and Special-Category Information

TradeEase does not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary, lawful and supported by an appropriate legal basis. If identity, verification, financial, biometric, health-related or other specially protected information becomes necessary for a particular service, we will provide appropriate notices and apply additional safeguards required by applicable law.

4. How We Use Personal Information

  • Create and administer accounts and vendor stores.
  • Process orders, payments, refunds, cancellations, fulfilment and delivery.
  • Communicate with customers, vendors and service providers about transactions and support.
  • Verify vendors, prevent fraud, detect abuse, protect users and maintain marketplace integrity.
  • Operate, maintain, troubleshoot and improve the Services.
  • Personalise marketplace experiences, recommendations and relevant content where permitted.
  • Send service messages, security alerts and administrative communications.
  • Send marketing communications where permitted by law and subject to applicable consent/opt-out requirements.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, accounting and law-enforcement obligations.
  • Establish, exercise or defend legal claims and protect TradeEase, users and the public.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where required by applicable law, TradeEase will rely on one or more lawful bases, including performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests, consent, and protection of vital interests where applicable. Where consent is used, it will be requested in a clear manner and may be withdrawn without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

TradeEase may use essential cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, shopping-cart functions and core operation. With appropriate notice and consent where legally required, TradeEase may also use analytics, performance, advertising or personalisation technologies. Users may control cookies through supported browser/device settings and any consent-management tools TradeEase provides.

7. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, including providers such as Paystack or other processors integrated into TradeEase. Payment providers may collect and process payment information under their own terms and privacy notices. TradeEase generally receives transaction confirmations, references and relevant payment metadata rather than full card credentials. Users should review the applicable payment provider’s privacy documentation before paying.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose personal information to vendors involved in fulfilling transactions; delivery/logistics partners; payment processors; hosting, cloud, security, analytics and communications providers; identity/verification providers; professional advisers; regulators and law-enforcement authorities where legally required; and parties involved in a corporate transaction such as merger, acquisition or restructuring.

TradeEase does not sell personal information merely because it shares necessary information with service providers or vendors to operate a transaction. Any advertising/marketing disclosure will be handled according to applicable law and the choices made available to users.

9. International Data Transfers

TradeEase may use service providers or infrastructure located outside the country where a user lives. Where applicable law regulates international transfers, TradeEase will use a legally recognised transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards, such as adequacy decisions, contractual safeguards or another permitted mechanism.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including contractual, accounting, tax, security, dispute-resolution and legal requirements. Retention periods may differ by data category. When information is no longer required, it will be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of, subject to lawful exceptions.

11. Security

TradeEase uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature and risk of the information, which may include encryption in transit, access controls, authentication controls, logging, backups, monitoring, secure development practices and least-privilege administration. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Your Privacy Rights

  • Access and obtain information about personal data we process about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion where legally available.
  • Request restriction of processing in applicable circumstances.
  • Object to certain processing, including direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability where legally applicable.
  • Complain to the competent data-protection authority.
  • Challenge certain solely automated decisions/profiling where applicable.

To exercise a right, contact privacy@tradease.ng. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Rights may be limited by applicable law, including where information must be retained for legal obligations or to establish/defend claims.

13. Children and Age Requirements

TradeEase is intended for users who are legally permitted to enter into marketplace transactions under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where doing so is prohibited. If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to applicable requirements, contact us so that we can assess and take appropriate action.

14. Third-Party Links and Services

The Services may contain links, integrations or content controlled by third parties. TradeEase is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third parties. Their own privacy notices and terms apply.

15. Vendor Privacy Responsibilities

Vendors are independent marketplace participants and may process customer information to fulfil orders, provide services, meet legal requirements and handle legitimate support needs. Vendors must process customer information lawfully, use it only for appropriate purposes, maintain reasonable security and comply with applicable data-protection requirements. Vendors must not use customer information for unsolicited marketing or unrelated purposes without a lawful basis.

16. Data Breach and Security Incidents

If TradeEase becomes aware of a personal-data incident, we will assess and respond according to applicable law, including any required notification to regulators and affected individuals. Users should promptly report suspected account compromise or security incidents to privacy@tradease.ng

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy when our Services, legal obligations or processing practices change. The updated version will be published with a revised “Last Updated” date. Where law requires, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.

18. Complaints and Regulatory Rights

Users may first contact TradeEase at privacy@tradease.ng. Where applicable, users may also complain to the data-protection authority in their country or region. Nigerian users may contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission through its official channels.
